/ Handover and shipping

How to hand over and collect a document

A photo is enough for a quote. The original is needed only once the order is paid and in progress – an apostille goes on the original or on an official copy, never on a photocopy. Four routes, all of them both ways.

In person – Warsaw,
City Centre, Krucza St.

We meet on the same street where the MFA accepts documents for legalisation – the document goes straight to the office. Place and time are agreed when you order, usually the same or the next working day.

We also come to Mokotów, Wola, Ochota, Żoliborz and Praga, and outside Warsaw we serve Piaseczno, Pruszków, Ząbki, Marki and Legionowo, among others.

In another city
or abroad?

  • Poland – InPost parcel locker WAW239M
  • Abroad – DHL Express
  • Ukraine – Nova Poshta

We receive the original and return the finished document the same way, with a tracking number.

Message us on WhatsApp

What an order looks like – five steps

  1. Photo or scan. Send it on WhatsApp, Telegram or by e-mail. Tell us what the document is for and in which country.
  2. Quote. We usually reply within 15 minutes: final price including official fees, deadline and order of steps (apostille first, then translation).
  3. Payment up front. BLIK, bank transfer or cash at the meeting; details come in the same message.
  4. Confirmation. A screenshot from your bank is enough – from that moment the order is in progress.
  5. Original and collection. Hand over the original at a meeting or ship it. You get a scan of the finished document right away and collect the original in person or by courier.

/ Where

Where in Warsaw apostille is issued

There is no single counter. Which office the document goes to depends on who signed it – and each office has different hours, a different fee and a different deadline. Below are the two Warsaw addresses we visit with our clients' documents.

Ministry

MFA

Legalisation Section of the Polish Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MSZ)

Address
ul. Krucza 38/42
00-512 Warszawa
Admission
by prior appointment only, with a printed confirmation
Fee
PLN 60 per document

Which documents go here

  • Civil status record — birth, marriage, death; issued by the registry office (USC)
  • Criminal record certificate — from the National Criminal Register (KRK)
  • Sworn translation — original with the translator's signature only; no apostille on a photocopy
  • Company documents — company registry extract (KRS), contracts, commercial documents
Courts

District Court

Certification of the signature of a notary from its district and of court documents

Address
al. „Solidarności” 127
room 20, ground floor, corridor G
Hours
Mon 8:30–18:00
Tue–Fri 8:30–15:30
Fee
PLN 26 per signature · min. 2 working days

Which documents go here

  • Notarial document — power of attorney, notarial deed, certification; from here the document still goes to the MFA for the apostille (another PLN 60)
  • Judgment, decision, court copy — judges' signatures and court seals

The order matters. A document from a notary is first certified by the District Court and only then goes to the MFA. Filed straight at the ministry, it comes back without the stamp – and that is a lost week.

Official fees are already included in our prices – you do not pay them separately at the counter. Offices change their hours, so we check before every visit. As of September 2026.

/ FAQ

Handover, shipping and payment – frequently asked questions

Do I have to come in person?

+

No. A scan or a photo on WhatsApp is enough for a quote. You hand over the original at a meeting in central Warsaw or send it via InPost locker WAW239M. From abroad we accept DHL Express, from Ukraine Nova Poshta; the finished document goes back the same way.

Can I come to your office?

+

We have no reception office – all contact is by phone and messengers, and documents change hands at an arranged meeting. We meet in the city centre on Krucza St. because the MFA legalisation section is there and the document goes straight to the office. That way you pay for the case, not for premises.

How do I send a document via an InPost locker?

+

Once the order is paid, we send you the ready details: destination locker WAW239M, the recipient's phone number and e-mail. Put the document in a stiff envelope so it does not bend. Send us the parcel number in a message – from then on we track it together with you. We return the finished document to the locker you choose.

How do I send a document from abroad or from Ukraine?

+

From EU and non-EU countries – by DHL Express courier, from Ukraine – by Nova Poshta. We give you the address after the quote, always with a tracking number both ways. Return shipping is included in the quote, so there is nothing to add at the end.

How and when do I pay?

+

Up front, after accepting the quote – by BLIK, bank transfer or cash at the handover. The quoted amount is final and includes official and court fees and shipping. The order goes into work once payment is confirmed – a screenshot from your banking app is enough.

When do you send the finished document?

+

We send a scan of the finished document as soon as we collect it from the office. The original is dispatched the same working day if it is ready by 15:00, otherwise the next morning. Personal collection is arranged for the nearest convenient time.

Do you work on Saturdays and public holidays?

+

We read messages every day and reply on working days 7:00–21:00, on Saturdays by arrangement. The offices – MFA, courts, registry offices – are closed at weekends and on public holidays, so the case does not move on those days. The calendar above on this page shows which days drop out.

Is it a bot replying?

+

No. Messages are read and answered by a person – the same one who later handles the case at the office. We do not use bots or artificial intelligence to process documents: civil records, diplomas and criminal record certificates contain personal data, and the responsibility is too great to hand to a machine.

Which language can I write in?

+

English, Polish, Ukrainian or Russian – we reply in the language you write in. The quote and payment details come in the same language.
