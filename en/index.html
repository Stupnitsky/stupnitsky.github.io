Sworn translation

Done by a translator on the Polish Ministry of Justice register. It carries a seal, a register number and a certification formula – which is why offices, courts, banks and universities accept it. Needed wherever a document has legal effect. If the office requires a translation from the original, you show the original when handing over or collecting documents.

Consular translation

A document prepared to a specific consulate's requirements or certified by a consul – for visa and passport matters, consents for a child to travel and powers of attorney. Requirements differ, so before we start we ask what the consulate expects.

A photo of the document is enough for a quote – we usually reply within 15 minutes.

/ Where

Where in Warsaw Apostille is issued

There is no single counter. Civil status records, criminal record certificates, company registry extracts and sworn translations go to the MFA at Krucza 38/42. Notarial and court documents are first certified by the District Court at al. „Solidarności” 127 and only then reach the ministry. We go to both addresses with our clients' documents – official fees are already in our prices.
