Sworn translation

Made by a sworn translator on the Minister of Justice's list. It carries the translator's seal, an entry number in the translator's register and a statement certifying that it matches the original – which is why offices, courts, banks and universities accept it. You need one wherever a document has to have legal effect. If the office requires a translation made from the original, you show it to us at a meeting or send it by parcel locker for inspection.

Ordinary translation

Stamped by a regular translator rather than a sworn one – for visa applications, correspondence, CVs and your own use. Polish offices and courts will not accept it. It costs 80 zł per page, and here a page means 1,800 characters with spaces; the turnaround is the same as for a sworn translation.

A photo of the document is enough for a quote – we usually reply within 15 minutes.

/ Where

Where in Warsaw to get an Apostille

There is no single counter. Civil status records, criminal record certificates, company registry extracts and sworn translations go to the MFA at ul. Krucza 38/42. Notarial and court documents are first certified by the Regional Court – at al. „Solidarności” 127 or, for the right bank of the Vistula, at ul. Poligonowa 3 – and only then go on to the ministry. We take our clients' documents to all three addresses, and the official fees are already included in our prices.
