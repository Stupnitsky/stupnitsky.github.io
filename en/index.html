/ Documents

Which document do you have?

Each document comes with the price of its Apostille or translation – final, official fees included. Not sure which one is yours? Send a photo and we’ll tell you in the quote.

Birth, marriage or death certificate

260,- zł

The MFA issues the Apostille on a paper copy from a Polish registry office (USC) – full, abridged or multilingual, but never on a photocopy. Getting married abroad? An Apostille on a copy of your birth certificate and on a certificate of capacity to marry or of marital status costs 520 zł with us for the two.

Apostille for civil status records

Power of attorney and notarial deed

380,- zł

A notary’s signature is first certified by the Regional Court for the place where the notary’s office is, and then the MFA issues the Apostille. If the notary is from Warsaw or the surrounding area, we handle both offices – usually within 3–5 working days. If the notary is from another city, you deal with the court locally and we get the Apostille at the MFA for 260 zł. A consent for a child to travel, a divorce judgment and an inheritance decision follow the same route.

Apostille via the Regional Court and the MFA

Company and other documents

260,- zł or 380,- zł

Whether a document goes straight to the MFA or to the Regional Court first depends on the signature, not the content – we’ll check it from a photo. A document signed by an official, such as a paper KRS extract or a criminal record certificate you already have, goes straight to the MFA and costs 260 zł with us. Articles of association, a resolution or a power of attorney from a notary go through the court – 380 zł in total. For countries outside the convention, such as the UAE or Egypt, we arrange legalisation at the MFA for the price of an Apostille, and you then go to the consulate yourself. A printout of an electronic document won’t get an Apostille.

Can you get an Apostille for documents from Ukraine?

+

Yes, and you don't have to leave Poland: an Apostille on a civil status record issued after 2016, a diploma or a school certificate costs 550 zł. A record issued before 2016 follows a different route – it is described on the page about documents from Ukraine. Scenarios, timelines and FAQ.

How long does an Apostille take in Warsaw?

+

Usually 1–3 working days at the MFA, and with the express service we file the document the same day, provided we have the original and proof of payment by 11:00. Notarial and court documents first go through the Regional Court, which takes at least two working days. More about timelines.

Do I have to come in person?

+

No. A photo is enough for a quote, and you hand over the original at a meeting in central Warsaw or send it via an InPost parcel locker. Handover and shipping.

Can I order several documents at once?

+

Yes, that is the most common case. Each document costs what the price list says, but the whole order gets one quote, one payment and one deadline. For 6 or more pages in one order we give 10% off translations, both sworn and ordinary.
