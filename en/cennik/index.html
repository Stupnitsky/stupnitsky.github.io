/ FAQ

Frequently asked questions Prices

Why does an Apostille cost 260 zł if the stamp duty at the MFA is 60 zł?

+

The stamp duty is 60 zł and it is included. The rest is our work: booking a slot in the MFA system – the first free one is often days away – two visits to Krucza, filing and collecting, checking the document before the order, a scan of the finished document straight away, and a reply with the price usually within 15 minutes. On your own: 60 zł, an online booking, two trips to the office during working hours and time off work. With us: a photo on WhatsApp and 1–3 working days.

Can the price in the quote change?

+

No. The amount in the quote is the final amount – that is what you pay and what you see in the confirmation. It changes only when the document turns out to be different from the photo: more pages to translate, or another type of document. We tell you before we order anything.

What counts as one document?

+

One document is one signature of an official, a notary or a translator – and one Apostille clause. A diploma and its supplement are two documents (2 × 260 zł). A notarial deed with annexes bound by the notary is one. A Sworn Translation of several documents means several translations, each with its own translator’s clause.

When and how do I pay?

+

After you accept the quote, up front: BLIK, bank transfer or cash at the meeting on Krucza. The payment details come with the quote. The order starts once the payment clears – we count the deadline from that day. Details in §4 of the Regulamin (PL).

How much is an Apostille in express mode and what are the conditions?

+

350 zł per document. We issue it the same day if the original reaches us by 11:00 together with the payment confirmation and needs no translation before the Apostille. Notarial and court documents have no express option – they need at least 2 working days at the District Court first.

How much do several documents in one order cost?

+

The same as each of them separately – documents in one order are not cheaper, but they are one quote, one payment and one deadline. The most common sets have their own lines in the price list: a set for a wedding abroad 520 zł, a Polish diploma with its supplement 520 zł, a company set 1 220 zł. Sworn Translation is charged separately, 90 zł per page, and from 6 pages in one order there is a 10% discount.

What if the office refuses the Apostille?

+

For a Polish document we refund the payment less the 60 zł stamp duty, which the MFA takes on filing and does not return. That is why we check the document before we accept the order – and say plainly when an Apostille cannot be issued: a plain photocopy, a laminated document, a Ukrainian record issued before 2016. For documents from Ukraine, if the archive refuses to issue a duplicate, we refund the prepayment – less the cost of shipping.
