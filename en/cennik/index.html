Frequently asked questions Prices

Can the price in the quote change?

+

No. The amount in the quote is final – that is what you pay and what you see in the confirmation. It changes only if the document you bring to the meeting turns out to be different from the photo: more pages to translate, or a different type of document. In that case we tell you before we go ahead with anything.

What counts as one document?

+

One document means one signature of an official, a notary or a translator – and one Apostille. A birth certificate and a certificate of marital status are two documents (2 × 260 zł). A notarial deed with annexes bound together by the notary is one. A sworn translation of several documents means several translations, each with its own translator’s certification.

When and how do I pay?

+

Up front, once you accept the quote: BLIK, bank transfer or cash at a meeting in Śródmieście. The payment details come with the quote. We start work once we receive confirmation of payment – the turnaround time runs from that day, and for an Apostille from the day we also have the original. Details in §4 of our Terms of Service (in Polish).

How much is an express Apostille and what are the conditions?

+

350 zł per document. We file it at the MFA the same day if the original reaches us by 11:00 together with proof of payment and does not need translating before the Apostille. Notarial and court documents have no express option – they need at least 2 working days at the Regional Court first. Sometimes the MFA has to verify a document with the office that issued it – for example, a certificate from a vehicle registration office or a disability certificate. In that case the Apostille takes longer, from a week to as much as a month. We have no influence on this stage: we file the document straight away and let you know about any delay the same day.

How much will I pay for several documents in one order?

+

The same as for each of them separately – an Apostille is not cheaper as part of a set, but the whole order has one quote, one payment and one turnaround time. The most common sets have their own lines in the price list: a set for a wedding abroad at 520 zł and a company set at 1 220 zł. Sworn translation is charged separately, at 90 zł per page, and from 6 pages in one order you get 10% off the translation, ordinary translations included.

What if the office refuses the Apostille?

+

For a Polish document, we refund the full payment, including the 60 zł official fee – we claim that fee back from the city office ourselves. That is why we check the document before we accept the order – and tell you plainly when an Apostille cannot be issued: a plain photocopy, a laminated document, a Ukrainian record issued before 2016. For documents from Ukraine, if the archive refuses to issue a duplicate, we refund the prepayment, minus the cost of shipping.

Why does an Apostille cost 260 zł if the official fee at the MFA is 60 zł?

+

The official fee is 60 zł and it is included. The rest is our work: booking a slot in the MFA system – the first free one is often several days away – two visits to the MFA on Krucza Street to file and collect, checking the document before you order, a scan of the finished document straight away, and a quote usually within 15 minutes. On your own: 60 zł, an online booking and a visit to the office during working hours, which usually means a day off work – and with a notarial document, two more visits to the court. With us: a photo in the quote form and 1–3 working days.
